    Welcome to Tiny Lives

    A little house where a family (and lots of animals) live their days, the seasons and a whole life story. There are no scores and no way to lose: watch, click around and discover things.

    How to play

    1. Click a room to peek inside. Click again, or press Esc, to see the whole house.
    2. Click anyone to say hi. They'll tell you what they're up to and play their own little tune.
    3. Time passes by itself. A day lasts about 4 minutes. Use 4× or 12× to speed up, or Space to pause.
    4. Jump around with the buttons under the picture: a time of day, a season, Next holiday ▸, Rainy day ▸, Snowy day ▸ or Life story ▸.
    5. The weather changes by itself with the seasons: sunny days, rain, storms, and snow in winter.
    6. The Right now list shows what everyone is doing. Click a name to find them.
